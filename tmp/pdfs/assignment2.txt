CSCI 565: Assignment 2

This is an individual assignment. Do your own work. Do not copy / paste anything from any outside source.
You may brainstorm with others on the high-level approaches if needed. It is intended that you figure this all
out on your own, but if you get stuck, and have put in a full effort to resolve your issues on your own, you may
come to office hours to get help if needed. My help will be brief and high level, giving a hint to point you in
the right direction.

Objectives
   • Starting with your assignment 1 work, create a multi-page HTML web site served from your local file
       system, with a navigation bar and separate pages for: The home page (the chess board), a History
       page, a Rules page, an About page.
   • Generate the chessboard from JSON data accessed after page load via AJAX, in both vanilla Javascript
       and JQuery.
   • Add a form under the chessboard and implement piece movement using DOM manipulation, both in
       vanilla Javascript and JQuery.
