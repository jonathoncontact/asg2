How to Play Chess

  • Pawn - Move forward one square or two on their first move. Capture diagonally.
  • Rook - Move horizontally or vertically as many squares as you want.
  • Knight - Move in an L-shape: two squares in one direction and then one square perpendicular. Can jump over pieces.
  • Bishop - Move diagonally any number of squares.
  • Queen - Move horizontally, vertically, or diagonally any number of squares.
  • King - Move one square in any direction. The king is the most important piece.
  • Castleing - A special move involving the king and a rook.
